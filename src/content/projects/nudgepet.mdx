<h1 slot="header">
  <span style="color: var(--darkAccent)">Nudge Pet</span> is a mixed reality widget that helps you stay aware of your mental and emotional state in instances where you are vulnerable. 
</h1>
<div slot="overviewDescription">
  This project was created at the **MIT Reality Hack 2025**
  hackathon, where our team was deeply inspired by one of our teammate's emotional 
  support animal (ESA). Really, we simply wanted to **have fun** at this hack and build something 
  we could all be proud of, and we did just that!
  <br />
  Because ESAs are not accessible to everyone, we wanted to create a virtual version that could provide the same benefits. We used 
  the **MetaQuest 3** to create a **mixed-reality** experience that dynamically responds to your stress levels that 
  are detected through **OpenBCI's EmotiBit**.
</div>

</Overview>

<ProblemSolutionStatement>
  <p slot='problem'>
    **43%** of all behaviors are habitual, and these habits are formed by a cue, a routine, and a reward. In moments of 
    stress or anxiety, it can be difficult to break these habits and form new ones. In these vulnerable moments, it's 
    easy to **mindlessly** fall back into old habits that are detrimental to our mental health.
  </p>

  <p slot="solution">
    Nudge Pet is a **mixed reality widget** that helps you stay aware of your mental state in instances where you are vulnerable. 
    By providing a **virtual ESA** that dynamically responds to your stress levels, we aim to help you stay aware of your mental state 
    and **nudge you towards healthier habits**.
  </p>
</ProblemSolutionStatement>

<PageSection>
  <h2 slot="sectionTitle">Research</h2>
  <div slot="description">
    Our research for NudgePet focused on two key areas: the psychological **benefits of human-animal interaction** 
    and the use of **physiological signals to detect emotional states**.
    <br />
    ### Human-Animal interaction
    Studies have shown that animals, particularly pets, can provide significant 
    <a href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5800290/" target="_blank">emotional support and stress relief for humans</a>. 
    This is partly due to the perception of animals as 
    <a href="https://vhil.stanford.edu/publications/augmented-reality/advantages-virtual-dogs-over-virtual-people-using-augmented-reality" target="_blank">non-judgmental entities</a>, 
    which allows people to confide in them more easily. The unconditional acceptance provided by animals can be beneficial when human relationships are affected by mental illness. 
    Animals can act as <a href="https://pdxscholar.library.pdx.edu/cgi/viewcontent.cgi?article=2785&context=honorstheses" target="_blank">"social catalysts"</a>, 
    helping pet owners form more friendships and social connections. 
    <br/>
    In the context of virtual pets, studies have demonstrated that interaction with computer-simulated animals can 
    <a href="https://www.researchgate.net/publication/280764411_Interacting_with_a_Computer-Simulated_Pet_Factors_Influencing_Children's_Humane_Attitudes_and_Empathy" target="_blank">promote empathy and positive attitudes in children</a>. 
    Virtual pets have also been used successfully to encourage healthy 
    behaviors and provide comfort in <a href="https://www.scientificarchives.com/public/assets/articles/article-pdf-1665943119-649.pdf" target="_blank">therapeutic settings</a>.
    <br/>
    ### Physiological signals
    To accurately assess users' emotional states, we focused on three key physiological signals:
    <br/>
    **Electrodermal Activity (EDA):** EDA has been shown to be one of the <a href="https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0166432817314006" target="_blank">most effective measures</a> 
    of anxiety, emotional status, cognitive status, and physiological activation. It measures changes in the electrical 
    properties of the skin related to sweat gland activity.
    <br/>
    **Photoplethysmography (PPG):** PPG can provide information on heart rate, respiration, oxygen levels, and hydration. 
    While <a href="https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC5900369/" target="_blank">not as precise as ECG</a> for heart 
    rate variability (HRV) measurements, it offers a good balance between accuracy and 
    convenience for wearable applications.
    <br/>
    **Body Temperature:** Measured using a thermistor, body temperature can provide 
    <a href="https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/veterinary-science/articles/10.3389/fvets.2023.1102937/full" target="_blank">additional context for emotional state assessment</a>. 
    <br/>
    Research has shown that combining these physiological signals can provide a comprehensive picture of an individual's 
    stress levels and emotional state. By integrating these research findings into NudgePet, we aimed to create 
    a virtual companion that could effectively detect users' emotional states and provide appropriate support, 
    leveraging the psychological benefits of human-animal interaction in a digital format.
  </div>
</PageSection>
